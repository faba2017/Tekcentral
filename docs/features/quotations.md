# Quotations

## CONFIRMED

- No quotation system is implemented yet.
- B2B customers may need formal quotations.
- Phase 3 product pages include a UI-only `Request a Business Quote` action that routes to the business page with product context.
- The business page provides a polished request entry point but does not create formal quotations yet.

## PLANNED

Quotations should be treated as a first-class business workflow.
